## Use case summary
Donate the Difference is a personal finance and giving app. Users opt in on our website, then text our number to log purchases they chose not to make (e.g. "croissant 5"). We reply to each message with a receipt showing progress toward the user's personal savings goal and the amount pledged to a matched charity, send a sign-in code when they log in, and send one weekly recap on Sundays. All messages are to users who opted in via the web form; no marketing to third-party lists.

## Opt-in description
Users opt in at https://notbuying.org by entering their mobile number, checking an unchecked consent box ("Text me receipts and a weekly recap from Donate the Difference. Message frequency varies with how often you text us. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."), and submitting. Full terms at https://notbuying.org/sms.
