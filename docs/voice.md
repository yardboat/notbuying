# Voice: Donate the Difference

**Three words:** Warm. Sharp. Unbothered.
Warm thesis, punk mouth. We like you. We're mad at the ad machine, not at you.

## Principles
1. **Aim up, never down.** The joke is on the ad budget, the algorithm, the cart timer. Never on the user, never on people who shop, never on people who need help.
2. **Celebrate the no, don't police it.** A skip is a win, not penance. No guilt, no "should", no streak-shaming.
3. **Say the number.** Specific beats vague: "+$5", "31%", "$0.50 pledged". No hand-wavy impact.
4. **Short like a flyer.** Receipts fit one text (160 chars). Fragments are fine. One joke per message, max.
5. **Charity is a yes, not a rescue.** Name the cause plainly. No pity, no sad-child energy, no "the less fortunate".
6. **Plain mouth.** No emoji in SMS, no curly quotes, no corporate verbs (leverage, empower, journey).

## Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Intent 1, impulse 0." | "Great job resisting temptation!" |
| "$0.50 pledged to Feeding America." | "Help feed the hungry!" |
| "Direct cash, no strings attached." | "Cash for people in poverty." |
| "Go get it." | "You've earned a guilt-free treat." |
| "Screenshot it. Post it." | "Make your friends feel bad about their cart." |
| "Nothing logged this week." | "You failed to save this week." |

## Six example lines
- **Hero:** Every no is a yes to something.
- **Receipt:** No croissant. +$5. Bike: $155 of $500 (31%). $0.50 pledged to Feeding America (~5 meals). Somewhere an ad budget just wept.
- **Sunday:** Issue 03 is out. Seven nos. $86 not spent.
- **Quiet week:** Nothing logged. Next time you walk past something, text it.
- **Error:** Needs a real price above $0. Try: croissant 5
- **Goal reached:** GOAL REACHED. Go get it. Then text: new goal ...
